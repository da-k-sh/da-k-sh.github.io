My long-term intellectual motivation lies in mathematical foundations, especially directed type theory and related structures that may eventually be useful in understanding fundamental physics and quantum gravity.

At the same time, I try to keep my work grounded in concrete research problems with short- and medium-term outputs. This has led me to projects in cryptography, formal methods, information flow, multi-agent AI systems, and hardware/security, where I can develop mathematical maturity, technical depth, and a track record of completed work while staying close to the kinds of foundational questions that motivate me most.

In practice, I try to let the long-term ambition set the direction, but let immediate research problems impose discipline.
